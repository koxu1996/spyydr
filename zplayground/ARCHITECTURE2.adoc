Spyydr splits the crawler into small, well-defined components connected by an Engine orchestrator:

Engine — the conductor; enforces limits, concurrency, and flow.

Frontier — a queue of crawl requests (e.g., BFS), with backpressure.

Fetcher — HTTP client with timeouts/retries/backoff.

Parser — extracts and resolves links from HTML.

Policy — gates discovery/fetch (whitelist/blacklist/depth).

Canonicalizer — normalizes URLs to reduce duplicates.

Registry — fast deduplication of URLs.

RateLimiter — politeness control (global, per-host later).

Reporter — output events (stdout or JSON), plus final stats.

Types/Error — shared models and error handling.
